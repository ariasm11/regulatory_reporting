# Golden examples

`original` uses the independent boundary fixture from tests/test_siter.py and contains records 01–05, an exact PH/PJ credit threshold, cash threshold, negative checking balance, card threshold, term threshold and an opening event. One below-threshold account and one excluded customer do not appear. `replacement` is the complete same population at sequence 01. `no_activity` is a separate header-only scenario, not a replacement for the original example.

These are separate fictitious scenarios. Do not combine the folders into one filing. Numeric IDs are synthetic and must not be submitted to ARCA.
